We get the question a lot: *what makes you the ones to decide who's best?* Fair.
"Best tattoo artist in the city" is a bold claim to print, so here's the honest
machinery behind it. No mystery, no kingmaking — just the five things we look at
every single time.

## 1. Healed work, not fresh photos

A fresh tattoo looks incredible for about a week. The real test is what it looks
like in two years. We hunt down **healed** work — settled lines, color that held,
black that didn't go patchy. Anyone can shoot a glossy photo five minutes after
the last pass. We're judging the version you'll actually wear.

## 2. Line quality

Clean, confident, consistent linework is the foundation everything else sits on.
Wobble, blowouts, and uneven weight don't hide, no matter how good the color is.
It's the first thing we look at and the hardest thing to fake.

## 3. Range — and a lane

The best artists usually have a signature lane *and* the range to prove it's a
choice, not a limitation. A fine-line specialist who can also pack a bold
traditional rose is showing you real command of the machine. (If those terms are
new, our [field guide to tattoo techniques](/blog/tattoo-techniques-single-needle-whip-shading-stippling/)
breaks them down.)

## 4. Consistency across a body of work

One stunning piece can be luck. Fifty strong pieces in a row is skill. We look at
the whole portfolio — the Tuesday-afternoon walk-ins, not just the convention
showpieces.

## 5. Reputation and the room

Cleanliness, professionalism, how they treat clients, how the shop runs. A
spotless station and an artist people trust matters as much as the art — and
[it's something you can read for yourself](/blog/whats-in-the-tray-tattoo-station-tour/)
before you ever sit down.

## Want to be in the running?

That's the whole rubric. We don't take payment to feature anyone, and following
count doesn't move the needle. If you tattoo — or you know the person who should
be crowned in your city — send us the name, the city, and the portfolio.
