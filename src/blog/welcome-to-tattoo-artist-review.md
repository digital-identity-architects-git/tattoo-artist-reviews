There are thousands of cities in America and a great tattoo artist hiding in
nearly every one of them. Most of them never get the spotlight they deserve.
We're changing that.

## The mission

One artist per city. The **best** one. We study the work, the style, and the
reputation, then we build a page that brags on them as hard as they've earned.
Texas first — because that's home — but we're mixing in cities coast to coast so
nobody's left out. Your city could be next.

## What to expect

- **New reviews daily** while we get rolling.
- **A custom, branded page** for every artist we feature.
- **Real picks** — no pay-to-play, no filler.
