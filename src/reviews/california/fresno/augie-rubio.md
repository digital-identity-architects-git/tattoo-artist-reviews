The Central Valley doesn't always get the credit its artists deserve, but Fresno has a real scene — and **Augie Rubio** has been one of the names anchoring it for the better part of two decades. As the owner of Most Wanted Tattoo & Body Piercing, he's the reason Fresno collectors don't have to drive to the coast for top-tier work. He takes the crown here.

## Why he takes the crown

Longevity in tattooing is its own kind of proof. With well over 17 years behind the needle, Rubio has the experience that only comes from thousands of hours and an enormous range of clients. That depth shows up in the details: clean execution, smart design decisions, and the confidence to guide a client toward a piece that will actually age well rather than just look good on day one. Running a busy, established shop on top of tattooing means he's also built a room people trust — which, when the work is permanent, matters as much as raw skill.

## The style

Rubio and the Most Wanted team work custom, spanning everything from delicate fine-line pieces to bold, intricate designs with real visual weight. That flexibility makes the shop a strong fit whether you're after your first small tattoo or a large, detailed project. The constant is a custom approach — your idea, drawn for you, built to suit your body and your story.

## Booking & where to find him

Augie Rubio tattoos out of Most Wanted Tattoo & Body Piercing in Fresno. Stop in or reach the shop directly to see current work and talk through a booking. If you're in the Valley and want work that holds up, you don't need to leave town.
