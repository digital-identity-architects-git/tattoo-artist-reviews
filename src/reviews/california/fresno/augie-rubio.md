Some shops earn their reputation one tattoo at a time, year after year. That's the Most
Wanted Tattoo story. Augie Rubio owns and runs the Fresno staple on North Blackstone, a
family-operated shop that's become one of the most-reviewed names in town.

## Why he takes the crown

Longevity and trust are the whole pitch here. Most Wanted has been open 17-plus years, and
the volume of local reviews — well into the thousands — tells you how many Fresno residents
have walked out happy. Rubio keeps it in the family, with relatives on staff, and the shop
turns up regularly in local "best tattoo shops" roundups and on Fresno news segments.

## The style

Rubio's most consistently cited strength is realism, and the shop runs full-service from
there — color, black-and-grey, and custom work across the board. It's a place built to take
a walk-in idea and turn it into something clean and lasting.

## Booking & where to find him

Most Wanted Tattoo & Body Piercing is on North Blackstone Avenue in Fresno. The shop's
Instagram is the fastest way to see recent work and reach out about an appointment.
