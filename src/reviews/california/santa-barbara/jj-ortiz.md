Santa Barbara is a small city with high standards, and its tattoo collectors are no exception. **JJ Ortiz** has spent years meeting those standards, building **805 Ink** into the studio locals point newcomers toward. That word-of-mouth trust is exactly why he takes the crown here.

## Why he takes the crown

Ortiz founded 805 Ink back in 2008 and has kept it running as a clean, professional, board-certified studio ever since — the kind of place that takes the craft and the safety equally seriously. Staying power in a town this size means you can't coast on a single style or a lucky season; you earn your reputation one healed tattoo at a time. The shop's standing as the go-to for "anyone interested in the highest quality tattoo in the area" is the payoff for that consistency.

## The style

What makes Ortiz a true best-in-city pick is range. He and the 805 Ink team are well-versed across American traditional, black & grey, Japanese, lettering, color realism, and neo-traditional work. That breadth means the shop can do a tight traditional eagle in the morning and a flowing Japanese sleeve in the afternoon without missing a beat. If you're not sure which lane your idea belongs in, that versatility is a feature — they'll help you land it in the right style.

## Booking & where to find him

JJ Ortiz and 805 Ink book through the [shop website](https://www.805ink.com/), where you can browse the portfolio and start an inquiry. If you're on the Central Coast and want work that matches the town's quiet confidence, this is the room.
