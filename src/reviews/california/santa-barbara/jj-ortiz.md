JJ Ortiz is the 805. A Santa Barbara native, he opened 805 Ink downtown in 2008 straight
out of college and has spent the years since turning it into the anchor of the city's
custom-tattoo scene.

## Why he takes the crown

Ortiz's pedigree is the real flex: his tattoo lineage runs directly back to Freddy Negrete,
a pioneer of California's fine-line black-and-grey tradition. Two-plus decades in, he's not
just a tattooer — he's a builder of the local scene. He expanded into a second studio,
Golden Eagle Tattoo on State Street, and in 2024 he organized the inaugural Rose Garden
Tattoo Convention, drawing 65-plus artists from around the world to Santa Barbara's
first-ever tattoo convention.

## The style

He's known for single-needle and fine-line black-and-grey — delicate, controlled work in
the Negrete tradition, where every line has to land the first time. It's the kind of
tattooing that looks effortless and absolutely isn't.

## Booking & where to find him

Ortiz splits time between 805 Ink downtown and Golden Eagle, where he tends to tattoo
Fridays and Saturdays. Start through the 805 Ink site or Instagram to see the work and get
on the books.
