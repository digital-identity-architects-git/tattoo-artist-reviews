Santa Cruz has always had a creative, do-it-your-own-way streak, and its best tattooing reflects that — personal, custom, and allergic to cookie-cutter flash. **Good Omen Tattoo**, founded by **Aaron Cooper and Tim Buonagurio**, is the clearest example in town, which is why the shop takes the crown in Santa Cruz.

## Why they take the crown

Cooper and Buonagurio opened Good Omen in the summer of 2012, both already established as award-winning professional tattooers, and built the studio around a simple promise: custom, one-of-a-kind tattoos. No two pieces off the same stencil, no recycling someone else's idea onto your skin. That commitment is harder than it sounds — it means every client gets original design work — but it's exactly what a town like Santa Cruz wants, and it's earned the shop a long run of strong reviews and loyal collectors.

## The style

Because the whole model is custom, the range is wide: detailed illustrative work, clean black & grey, and color pieces, all drawn specifically for the person wearing them. The duo's award-winning backgrounds mean the fundamentals — solid linework, smart composition, tattoos built to age well — are never in question. Whether you arrive with a fully formed concept or just a feeling you want captured, the process starts with your idea and ends with something that exists nowhere else.

## Booking & where to find them

Good Omen Tattoo books through its [website](https://goodomentattoo.com/), where you can see the artists' portfolios and start a conversation. If you're in Santa Cruz and you want a tattoo that's genuinely yours, this is the shop.
