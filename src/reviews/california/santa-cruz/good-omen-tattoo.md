Not every crown goes to a single chair. In Santa Cruz, the name that keeps coming up is a
shop: Good Omen Tattoo on Water Street, a veteran-run studio that's been a fixture of the
local scene since 2012.

## Why it takes the crown

Good Omen opened in the summer of 2012, founded by two artists who between them had been
tattooing since around 1990 — 50-plus years of combined experience walking in the door on
day one. That depth shows in the reviews: the shop sits near a perfect rating across a
couple hundred of them. It's also run clean to a fault — a fully disposable, single-use
setup with no re-sterilization, which is exactly the kind of detail that separates a
serious studio from the rest.

## The style

The crew's strengths run to portraits, realism, and fantasy and illustrative work, with the
full range of custom styles on offer. Walk-ins are welcome and the doors are open seven
days a week — a rare thing for a shop doing work at this level.

## Booking & where to find them

Good Omen Tattoo is on Water Street in Santa Cruz, open daily. Check the shop's Instagram
or website for artists' portfolios and to plan a piece.
