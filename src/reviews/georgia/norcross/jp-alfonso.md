JP Alfonso has been chasing this craft almost his whole life. He started tattooing at
twelve in Havana, hunted down mentors at sixteen, emigrated to the U.S. in 2006, and went
fully professional in 2008. Today his studio in Norcross is one of the most in-demand
rooms in metro Atlanta.

## Why he takes the crown

The work speaks first. Within roughly a decade of going pro, Alfonso became a multi-award-
winning realism artist with pieces featured across tattoo magazines and the big industry
social accounts. JP Alfonso Studios has run for nearly a decade with a near-spotless
review record — hundreds of five-star ratings — and a waitlist that reportedly stretches
months out. People plan around getting tattooed by him.

## The style

His signature is black-and-grey realism — portraits and detailed compositions with smooth
gradients and real depth — but he ranges into color realism, surrealism, and neo-
traditional, and he's a go-to for ambitious cover-ups. The range is what keeps him at the
top: he can match the piece to the person instead of the other way around.

## Booking & where to find him

Alfonso works out of JP Alfonso Studios in Norcross, just outside Atlanta. Expect a wait —
that's the price of the best chair in town. Start through his Instagram or studio website.
