Baton Rouge has a steady, no-nonsense tattoo culture — collectors here want big, clean, lasting work, not gimmicks. **Bryan Loggins** has spent his career delivering exactly that out of one of the city's longest-running studios, and that's why he takes the crown.

## Why he takes the crown

Loggins works out of Body Images Tattoo Clinic, which has been serving Baton Rouge safely for nearly three decades — and that institutional longevity matters. It means a deep well of experience, a reputation built over years rather than a viral month, and the kind of trust that only comes from a community watching your work heal and hold up over time. Loggins specializes in the demanding stuff: black-and-grey realism and large projects that require both stamina and a real plan. Those are the pieces where experience separates the pros from the pretenders.

## The style

His focus is black & grey realism, large-scale sleeves, and neo-traditional work — the kind of ambitious, custom tattoos that turn an arm or a back into a single cohesive piece. If you've been sitting on the idea of a full sleeve or a big black-and-grey statement, Loggins is the type of artist you want steering it: someone who can map the whole composition, manage the flow across your body, and execute it cleanly from first session to last.

## Booking & where to find him

Bryan Loggins tattoos at Body Images Tattoo Clinic in Baton Rouge. Reach the shop directly to see current work and talk through your project. If you want a big, clean piece built to last, he's the call.
