When a tattoo shop survives a quarter-century in the same neighborhood, the artist behind
it is doing something right. Bryan Loggins co-owns Body Images Tattoo Clinic on Government
Street in Baton Rouge's Mid City — a fixture of the local scene and his base for the big,
detailed work he's known for.

## Why he takes the crown

Body Images has been inking Baton Rouge for well over two decades, and Loggins has helped
keep it on the city's "best of" lists the whole way. He runs a serious, professional
operation — formal consultations, booked appointments, real planning for real projects —
which is exactly what the large-scale pieces he favors demand.

## The style

His lane is black-and-grey realism and neo-traditional, built out at scale: full sleeves and
ambitious multi-session projects where the payoff is in the planning. This is work for
people who want to commit to a complete piece rather than collect one-offs.

## Booking & where to find him

Loggins books by appointment through Body Images Tattoo Clinic — by email and online, not
DMs. Start at the shop's website or his Instagram to see the portfolio and get the process
going.
