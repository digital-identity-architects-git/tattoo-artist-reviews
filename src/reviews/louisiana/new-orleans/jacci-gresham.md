New Orleans has one of the richest tattoo cultures in the country — Frenchmen Street alone could anchor a "best of" list. But the crown here goes to someone whose story is bigger than any single style: **Jacci Gresham**, widely credited as the first Black female tattoo artist in America, and the keeper of the city's oldest still-active tattoo shop.

## Why she takes the crown

Gresham opened Aart Accent in 1976, and it's been a New Orleans institution ever since — the oldest continuously operating tattoo shop in the city. That alone makes her a piece of living history. But longevity isn't the whole story: she's spent decades honing a craft she helped open doors in, building a body of work and a reputation that artists across the country know and respect. Choosing the "best in New Orleans" could spark a dozen arguments, but featuring a genuine pioneer who's still tattooing the city she helped shape isn't a hard call. It's an honor.

## The style

Gresham is celebrated for her classic, traditional sensibility and especially her lettering — her beautiful penmanship is a signature, and clean lettering is one of the truest tests of a tattooer's hand. Aart Accent has delivered traditional and custom Americana work for generations of New Orleanians, the kind of solid, timeless tattoos that look right the day you get them and decades later. This is heritage tattooing in the best sense.

## Booking & where to find her

Jacci Gresham tattoos at Aart Accent Tattoos in New Orleans. Reach the shop directly to talk through a piece. If you want ink from someone who helped write American tattoo history — and is still writing it — she's the call.
