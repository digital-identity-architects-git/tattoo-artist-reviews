The Gulf Coast has a tattoo culture all its own, and in Biloxi the name that anchors it is **Tom Donovan**. A master of color and a true artist, he didn't just build a great chair for himself — he built **Biloxi Ink Tattoos** into a destination. That's why he takes the crown here.

## Why he takes the crown

Donovan's reputation rests on two things. First, his own work: he's known across the coast as a master of color, the discipline that punishes weak technique the fastest, since vivid, saturated tattoos demand a sure hand to heal clean and stay bright. Second, his eye for talent — he's brought together a roster of award-winning artists at Biloxi Ink, the mark of someone who's thinking about the whole craft and the long-term health of the local scene, not just his own bookings. A shop is only as good as the standard its leader sets, and Donovan sets a high one.

## The style

Color is the headline — bright, bold, well-composed pieces that hold their punch — but the Biloxi Ink room covers custom work across the board, from realism to clever cover-ups that rescue old ink. Whether you want a vivid color design of your own or a fresh start over a tattoo you've outgrown, this is a studio with the range and the standards to deliver.

## Booking & where to find him

Tom Donovan and Biloxi Ink Tattoos book through the [shop website](https://biloxiinktattoos.com/), where you can browse the team's work and start an inquiry. If you're on the Mississippi coast and want color that stays loud, he's the call.
