Bold lines, solid color, designs built to read across a room and last a lifetime — that's
the traditional tattooing Tom Donovan has been doing on the Mississippi Gulf Coast for
three decades. From Biloxi Ink Tattoo, the shop he owns, he's a fixture of the local scene.

## Why he takes the crown

Donovan came up the fine-art way, with a BFA in painting and printmaking before he ever
picked up a machine — he first tattooed up in Calgary, learning from Paul Jeffries. Thirty-
plus years later, his standing in the traditional world is the tell: his flash designs are
collected and sold commercially through Tattoo Archive, the kind of recognition reserved
for artists whose drawings other tattooers want to put on their own walls.

## The style

He's a traditional and flash specialist — clean, classic American work with the confidence
that only comes from decades of repetition. At Biloxi Ink he's also assembled a crew of
strong local artists, so the shop covers plenty of ground, but Donovan's own lane is the
old-school foundation done right.

## Booking & where to find him

Biloxi Ink Tattoo sits on Pass Road in Biloxi. Check the shop's Instagram or website for
the latest flash and to set up an appointment.
