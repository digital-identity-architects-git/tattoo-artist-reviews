Las Vegas might have the highest tattoo-shop-per-block ratio in America, which means the bar for *best* is brutally high. **Joe Riley** clears it by doing the unglamorous thing in a flashy town: putting the art first. As the owner of Inner Visions Tattoo, he's built one of the most respected rooms in the valley — and that's why he takes the crown in Vegas.

## Why he takes the crown

Riley has been voted one of the best tattoo artists in Las Vegas, and Inner Visions has the award-winning team to match. But the reputation isn't built on trophies — it's built on a culture. Inner Visions is known as an artist-driven shop, the kind of place where the focus is on doing the work right rather than rushing tourists through the chair. In a city built on volume, choosing craftsmanship is a statement, and it's the reason serious collectors seek the studio out instead of grabbing the nearest neon sign.

## The style

Riley and his team are genuine generalists in the best sense — strong across black & grey, color, and realism, with the range to take on custom pieces of nearly any style. That versatility makes Inner Visions a smart first stop whether you're after a clean black-and-grey piece, a vivid color design, or a detailed realistic portrait. Everything runs through a custom, collaborative process, so the tattoo fits you rather than a template.

## Booking & where to find him

Inner Visions Tattoo books through its [website](https://www.innervisionstattoo.com/), where you can browse the team's portfolios and start an inquiry. If you're in Las Vegas and you want a shop that treats your skin like a canvas instead of a transaction, this is it.
