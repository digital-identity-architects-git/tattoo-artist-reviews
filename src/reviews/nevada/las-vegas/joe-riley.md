Biomechanical tattooing lives or dies on the illusion — the sense that there's chrome and
sinew working underneath the skin. Few artists sell it like Joe Riley. From Inner Visions
Tattoo in Las Vegas, the shop he owns, he's built a two-decade reputation as the city's
biomech specialist.

## Why he takes the crown

Riley has been tattooing for 20-plus years and is recognized as a leader in the
biomechanical niche — work that demands you sculpt depth, light, and anatomy convincingly
across a moving canvas. His shop bio cites work published across tattoo magazines and a
shelf of awards; whatever the exact tally, the result is plain in the portfolio, and Inner
Visions is regularly named among the best shops in town.

## The style

Biomechanical is the signature — gears, pistons, and torn-skin effects fitted to the body's
own contours — but he's a deep all-rounder in black-and-grey, realism, portraits, and
cover-ups. That range makes him a natural for the hardest jobs: turning an old mistake into
something that looks engineered on purpose.

## Booking & where to find him

Riley tattoos out of Inner Visions Tattoo in Las Vegas. Start with his Instagram or the
shop website to see the biomech work and reach out for an appointment.
