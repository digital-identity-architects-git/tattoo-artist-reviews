The Queen City has a serious tattoo lineage, and when the conversation turns to the best in Cincinnati, one name keeps surfacing: **Kore Flatmo**. He's been quietly setting the standard for portrait and realism work in this city for over two decades, and that's why he takes the crown.

## Why he takes the crown

Flatmo opened his appointment-only studio, PluraBella, in 1999, after breaking into the industry young and absorbing the craft from shops across the country. That obsessive, study-everything approach shows in the work: when it comes to portraiture and color realism, the studio is widely regarded as unmatched in the city. Going appointment-only is itself a tell — it's the move of an artist who'd rather pour deep focus into a smaller number of ambitious pieces than churn through walk-ins. The result is work with the patience and polish that realism demands.

## The style

Flatmo's reputation is built on portraits and color realism — faces, detail, and depth rendered with a painter's sensitivity — and extends into custom large-scale and Japanese-influenced compositions. This is the artist you seek out for an ambitious, planned piece: a memorial portrait, a detailed color sleeve, a backpiece you've been thinking about for years. The custom, by-appointment process gives the work the runway it needs.

## Booking & where to find him

Kore Flatmo tattoos by appointment at PluraBella in Cincinnati. Because the studio is appointment-only, plan ahead and reach out early to start the conversation. If you want realism that ranks with anything in the Midwest, he's the call.
