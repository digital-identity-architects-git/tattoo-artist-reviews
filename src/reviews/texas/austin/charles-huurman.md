Austin's tattoo scene runs deep — traditional, fine-line, Japanese, the whole spectrum. But every so often you see work that makes you lean in and ask *how is that on skin?* That's **Charles Huurman**, and that reaction is exactly why he takes the crown in Austin.

## Why he takes the crown

Huurman comes to tattooing from a fine-art painting background, and it shows in every piece. He's internationally recognized for realism — the kind of black-and-grey and color work that creates a genuine illusion of depth, as if the image is sitting *inside* the skin rather than on top of it. That painter's eye for light, shadow, and dimension is what separates a good realism tattoo from one that stops people in the street. His work has been recognized at conventions around the world, and he's tattooed a long list of high-profile clients, but the portfolio speaks louder than any résumé.

## The style

If you want realism, Huurman is the call. He works in hyper-realistic black & grey, color realism, and abstract designs that lean into 3D illusion — portraits, nature, surreal compositions, and pieces that play with the eye. This is detail-driven work that rewards a big canvas and a real plan, so expect a thoughtful, custom process rather than a walk-in flash grab. The trade-off for that patience is a tattoo that holds its drama for years.

## Booking & where to find him

Charles Huurman tattoos out of his Austin studio, and the best place to see current work and start a booking conversation is his [shop site](https://www.charleshuurmantx.com/). If you're in Austin and you want a piece that looks like it could climb off your arm, he's worth the wait.
