Charles Huurman paints with a tattoo machine. Raised between Ireland and Spain by an
oil-painter father, he came to skin from a fine-art background — and it shows in every
piece. Now based in a private studio on Austin's East 6th, he's one of the city's premier
realism specialists.

## Why he takes the crown

Huurman started tattooing professionally in 2008, trading a painter's canvas for a living
one. What followed is one of the more decorated résumés in modern realism: dozens of
awards (his own count runs north of fifty) across major international conventions from New
York to Madrid, Milan, and Hong Kong, plus guest spots and seminars at renowned studios
around the world. The recognition is earned — his color work holds depth and light that
most realism never reaches.

## The style

He's best known for hyper-real color portraits and what he calls abstract realism: a fusion
of photographic accuracy with the energy and movement of abstract painting. Splashes,
drips, and brush-stroke texture wrap around faces and figures so the whole piece feels
alive rather than copied. He works in black-and-grey too, but the painterly color is his
signature.

## Booking & where to find him

Huurman tattoos by appointment out of his private Austin studio. Start with his Instagram
or his website to see the portfolio and get on the books.
