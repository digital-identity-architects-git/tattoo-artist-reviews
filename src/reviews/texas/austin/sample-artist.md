Austin's tattoo scene runs deep — but every so often you find someone whose line
work makes the rest of the room go quiet. **Mars Vega** is that artist.

## Why she takes the crown

Mars builds tattoos the way an architect builds a façade: every line is
intentional, every negative space earns its place. Her fine-line blackwork holds
up *years* later — no blowouts, no muddy healing — which is the real test nobody
talks about. Walk into Lone Star Ink Co. and you'll see a wall of healed work
that looks like it was done yesterday.

## The style

She's best known for botanical and neo-traditional pieces — think delicate
linework wrapped around bold, confident shapes. It's the kind of range that lets
her do a dime-sized sprig of rosemary in the morning and a full neo-traditional
panther sleeve in the afternoon.

## Booking & where to find her

Mars books through her shop and her Instagram. If you're in Austin and you want
work that ages like a tattoo *should*, she's the call.
